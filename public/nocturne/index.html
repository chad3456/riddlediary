Our thesis

Open networks beat closed platforms over a long enough time horizon. We invest where ownership, settlement, and coordination are moving on-chain — and we stay through the cycles it takes to prove it.

01

Infrastructure

Base layers, sequencers, data availability, and the tooling that lets builders ship without reinventing the stack.

02

Markets

Exchanges, credit, and derivatives protocols that move value with fewer intermediaries and less friction.

03

Consumer

Wallets, identity, and applications that make ownership legible to people who have never heard the word "blockchain."

04

Research

Cryptography, mechanism design, and the open questions that determine what's even possible to build next.

How we work

  1. 01

    We write the check ourselves

    The partner you pitch is the partner in your board meetings, from term sheet through exit.

  2. 02

    We publish our thinking

    Our research is public by default — if an idea is worth funding, it's worth arguing for in the open.

  3. 03

    We size for conviction, not FOMO

    Fewer bets, larger ownership, and reserves held back for the rounds that matter most.

  4. 04

    We think in cycles, not quarters

    Crypto rewards patience. We underwrite theses that take years to play out, not months.
