Indicator

Every value is loaded from a generated dataset built by scripts/build_infra.py from vendored source files; no figure is entered by hand. Each indicator carries its publisher, source URL, and a data-quality score derived from provenance, corroboration, completeness and recency.

Population denominators for per-capita and per-million figures come from the World Bank population series, cross-checked against Our World in Data. Both are shown in the indicator's source panel.
