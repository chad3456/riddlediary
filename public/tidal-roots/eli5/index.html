This is a stretch of river delta — flat, low, and laced with tidal creeks.

Thousands of households here live within a few hundred metres of the water.

Without a root system to hold it, the bank simply washes away — a little more with every monsoon.

Right now

Every high tide chews a little further into the bank. Nothing is holding the silt in place.

What changes

Mangrove roots go back into the mud. Living root systems knit the silt together from below.

What that buys

The same tide arrives — and the bank simply holds. That's the whole idea.

That's it. Roots hold ground.
Ground holds carbon.
